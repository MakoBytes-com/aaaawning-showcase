### How we improved SEO (in plain English)

We made every change Google and AI search engines look for:

- **Each page has unique, keyword-rich content** targeted at real search queries
- **Structured data** that tells Google exactly what AAA Awning does and where — so Google can show rich results (star ratings, business hours, services) in search
- **Every image has descriptive filename + alt text** with location + product keywords
- **Site loads fast** (Google confirmed ranking factor)
- **Mobile-first** (Google now ranks mobile experience first)
- **All 64 pages submitted in a sitemap** so Google crawls the whole site efficiently
- **301 redirects** from every old WordPress URL to the new equivalent — so any old bookmarks, printed materials, or inbound links keep working
- **AI search optimization** — separate from Google, we optimized specifically for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. A surprising amount of commercial-customer research now happens through AI chat; we wanted AAA to show up there too
